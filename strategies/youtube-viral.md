# YouTube Viral Strategy (in the autopilot agent)

Source of truth: ~/workspace/social-growth/GROWTH-SYSTEM.md + GROWTH-ADDENDUM-YT-FB.md.
Where those and Rony's locked rules disagree, Rony's rules win.

## Hard targets (the agent scores against these, every week)
- Long-form CTR: 4-10%
- Long-form retention: 40-60%
- Shorts viewed-vs-swiped: >=80%
- Rony Cn hold rate: >60% (3-sec hold)
- 48-hour rule: a long-form video below CTR target at 48h gets a title/thumbnail rework, not a re-upload. Shorts do NOT get this rule.

## What the algorithm rewards (extracted, not guessed)
- CTR first: title + thumbnail decide the click. Title 50-60 chars, keyword first, 3-4 words max on thumbnail, one focal point.
- Retention second: hook in first 3-5s, one idea per video, jump cuts, visual change every 2-3s, progress hooks, loop ending back to the hook.
- Session: end screens + related-video funnel Shorts -> long-form ("Top 5 [Category] Under $X" compilations from the week's Shorts assets).
- Consistency over bursts: fixed cadence beats volume spikes. Deals Shorts engine (10/day) stands; Rony Cn stays 2 posts/day max, video at 20:00 +06, approval-gated.
- Upload discipline checklist (every upload): title formula, thumbnail rules, category playlist, pinned-comment question, subscribe CTA with a reason AFTER value, captions file, keyword file-name, affiliate disclaimer on Deals (tag rony2u-20).

## Topic picking (5-factor rubric, shareability gate)
Demand / Curiosity / Expertise / Ease / Shareability. If shareability fails, the topic fails, however good the rest is.
Mix: 70% evergreen / 30% trending (Deals). Rony Cn recurring segment: "সোমবারের AI Trick" Mondays 20:00.

## Excluded
Tiny Tales TV: fully out of automation and out of all YouTube tactics. Bangla Hub: Blogspot only, never YouTube.
Free-only: no paid boosts, ever.
